# Synchronize Planning Trackers

Match each tracker to its own page and current publication. Synchronize through the business tool and
inspect the resulting publication/version/decision references. A status label alone is insufficient.

After the initial delivery, poll the declared planning change window. If the environment announces an
amendment, inspect the updated source requirements and dependent pages. Reconcile and re-review only
affected documents, publish new artifacts and update their trackers. Unaffected branches may keep
their valid publications. Confirm every planned change window is drained before final delivery.

Outputs: synchronized tracker IDs and the publications used. Preserve obsolete references in history
for diagnosis instead of presenting them as current completion.
