# Reconcile publication identity

Read immutable acquisition records for candidate aliases. Normalize their authoritative DOI according
to the public rule, not mutable catalog titles. The optional scripts/identity_groups.py groups supplied
records only; it cannot read the workspace or approve a merge. Choose a canonical representative for each
proven same-work group. Either representative is legal. Merge and inspect the canonical record, existing
annotations, collections and citation network. A late alias can replace a representative used by earlier
skills; ownership must transfer without losing source contexts or frozen export history. Similar titles
with different authoritative identifiers must remain distinct. Apply released source revisions separately
when the chosen representative has older catalog metadata.
