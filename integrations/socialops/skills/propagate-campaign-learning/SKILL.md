# Propagate Campaign Learning

Use the completed brief report to prepare its deployment, retaining the winning content and audience.
After all wave-one deployments are published, poll the declared rights update. Inspect affected assets
and unpublished posts. Replace withdrawn assets and obtain new approval where necessary; do not rewrite
published history or restart unrelated campaigns.

For a later wave, recover the exact reports of every parent brief. At cross-campaign joins, compare
their winner click rates, retain every parent CTA and intersect consenting audiences with the local
allowed set. Referencing a similarly named report without carrying its measured content is insufficient.

Outputs: report/deployment references, applied-update evidence and the next wave's actual learning bindings.
