# Inspect Campaign Brief

Read the public task, campaign-wave brief and assigned post details. Note product facts, disclosure,
CTA alternatives, audience rules, asset readiness, account identity and release windows. Assets are
metadata-only objects; do not infer that image content has been visually reviewed.

For later waves, locate the actual completed reports of every declared parent brief. Inspect their
winner observations, content and source identities. Compare normalized click rates, not raw clicks.
History can include obsolete approvals and unrelated posts. Preserve report/post/version bindings.

Outputs: inspected briefs, posts and relevant historical report IDs. This workflow does not authorize
publication or select a gold next skill.
